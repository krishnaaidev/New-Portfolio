My Journey & Engineering Vision

My engineering journey began with an innate fascination for how computational algorithms learn from raw information to make high-stakes, intelligent decisions. Today, I specialize in Machine Learning, Deep Learning architectures (NLP, Computer Vision), Prompt & Generative AI, and Data Analytics pipelines that transform unorganized data into strategic, actionable outcomes.

As a Google Student Ambassador and active Google Developer Groups (GDG) Member, I regularly organize hands-on technical sessions, cloud workshops, and hackathons—having mentored 500+ students across AI adoption, Google Cloud tools, and software engineering fundamentals.

I believe software is most powerful when it touches lives directly: from agricultural diagnostics (Plant Disease Recognition) and healthcare screening (Multimodal Depression Detection) to institutional governance (CTG Faculty App) and autonomous hiring systems (AIHire).

Engineering Philosophy

"Code is only as valuable as the real-world impact it unlocks. Whether training multimodal psychiatric diagnostic models, engineering autonomous recruitment engines like AIHire, digitizing institutional KRA performance workflows, or optimizing commercial supply chain profitability, I design systems with end-users, ethical rigor, and measurable outcomes at the core."
